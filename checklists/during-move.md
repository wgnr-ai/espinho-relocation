# During-Move Checklist

## Travel
- [ ] Book flights Orlando → Porto (OPO)
- [ ] Arrange airport pickup/transport to Espinho
- [ ] Pack carry-on essentials (documents, medications, electronics)

## Orlando Handoff
- [ ] Complete property handoff (sale/lease/management)
- [ ] Cancel/subtransfer utilities
- [ ] Notify US postal service of forwarding
- [ ] Close or transfer subscriptions

## Logistics
- [ ] Confirm movers have correct Espinho delivery address
- [ ] Ensure shipment insurance is active
- [ ] Keep copies of all shipping documents

## Arrival
- [ ] Check into temporary or permanent housing
- [ ] Verify utilities are connected
- [ ] Locate nearest pharmacy and grocery store
- [ ] Contact family in area to announce arrival
