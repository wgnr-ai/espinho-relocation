# Shipping & Logistics

## Purpose
Planning the physical move of belongings from Orlando to Espinho.

## Ownership
Project-level; requires coordination with movers.

## Work Guidance
- Get quotes from 3+ international movers
- Returning citizens qualify for isenção de direitos aduaneiros (customs duty exemption)
- Create detailed bring-vs-buy inventory
- Note voltage difference: 110V (US) vs 230V (PT) for electronics
- Book movers 2-3 months in advance
